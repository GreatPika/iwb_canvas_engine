# Change Contract

## Goal

Direct sparse-store preparation uses one isolated transaction-local candidate that composes the existing authoritative family editor with transaction-owned resource and structural editors, replays every `StoreSparseMutation` in journal order, preserves current policy, diagnostics, acceptance, touched-fact, admission, and finalization semantics, normalizes and freezes each changed owner once, shares untouched immutable owners by identity, and exposes zero or one immutable `CommittedDocument` candidate without projection, intermediate committed candidates, recursive overlays, or mutation-multiplied owner work.

## Boundaries

Owner: `DocumentStoreKernel` owns the direct sparse preparation boundary and one private transaction candidate. The candidate composes the existing `FamilyTablesEditor` unchanged, a `ResourceTable` consume-once editor, a structural editor over `LayerTable`/background/content order, the shared store-side deterministic implicit AVL/order-statistics sequence implementation, scalar candidate facts, accepted/touched/admission/deferred ledgers, and final publication through `CommittedDocument`. `FamilyTables`, `ResourceTable`, `LayerTable`, `ElementRegistry`, `_IdAdmission`, and `CommittedDocument` retain their existing committed fact ownership. `docs/architecture/03_data_model.md`, the Store-finalization portion of `docs/contracts/edit_kernel.md`, and ADR-0017 own maintained description/rationale after the production cutover.
