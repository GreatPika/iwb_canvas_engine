Order Constraints: Change the public DTO and `SelectionDeleteFacts.availability` atomically with direct compile and behavioral evidence; update equality evidence and the normative public contract in the same unit; do not change the export-name registry or add a parallel runtime fact before or after the cutover.
Temporal Surface Closure: `CanvasSelectionPort.deleteAvailability` continues to re-read `selectionDeleteFacts()` on each access, and all three booleans in one returned value come from that single fact bundle; no cached availability or revision synchronization is introduced.
All-Or-Nothing Failure Boundary: Availability construction is a pure read with no mutation or callback; the unit either lands the complete public field, fallback, runtime mapping, equality/hash behavior, evidence, and normative contract together or does not land. Negative Proof And Fixture Quarantine: Normal public fixtures own the empty, deletable-only, mixed, and protected-only states; unresolved or non-content selection facts remain confined to the existing command-facts owner fixture and do not justify a production invalid-state route or new synchronization surface.
Bounded Recognition Scope: No analyzer, guardrail, scanner, generated-output recognizer, or new fixture-recognition mechanism is introduced; existing compile, runtime behavior, equality, static analysis, and documentation checks consume their established owners directly.
Work Budget And Cost Displacement: Construction/import/reset adds only assignment of the public boolean and no document work; mutation/update/replay remains unchanged because availability is derived and not stored; freeze/publication/install remains unchanged because the field is absent from runtime state and persistence; query/read performs only `deletableEntries.isNotEmpty` after the existing single projection and filter, with no additional whole-owner pass, lookup, or allocation proportional to selection or document size; cleanup/rollback remains unchanged because availability has no side effects; no work may be displaced into selection mutation, document mutation, commit installation, publication, or cleanup.
