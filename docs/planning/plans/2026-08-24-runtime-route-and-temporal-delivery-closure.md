# Change Contract

## Goal

Draw and line interaction commits obtain their generated element IDs without reserving them before accepted installation, so failed or no-op preparation leaves the next ID unchanged while successful and explicit generation retain their prior sequence, and every runtime-owned resolver, post-install route transition, text-listener interleaving, guarded spatial/resource/frame/state/action/observer delivery step, failure route, and public observation remains in its exact synchronous order over the already-completed Store, EditKernel, and CommitApplier atomic boundaries.
