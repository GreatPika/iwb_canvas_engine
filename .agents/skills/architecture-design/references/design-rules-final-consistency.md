# Architecture Design Final Consistency Rules

## 10. Final Consistency Check

After lint and before any terminal result, semantically reconcile the frontmatter
outcome and disposition with every `S/E/R/F/M/P/D/A/I/H/DG/B`, each derived view,
current repository fact, and Contract Interface projection.

Confirm all of the following:

- exactly one evidence-supported disposition and one canonical outcome owner exist;
- the frontmatter outcome points to that exact `R.Kind=outcome` under every
  disposition;
- every named source, user decision, accepted upstream decision, and exclusion is
  preserved once and used through typed canonical edges;
- `Impacts` equals the complete Impact Register `I` set and `Stops` equals the complete
  Stop Conditions `H` set, with neither field owning or repeating a record body;
- diagrams explain rather than redefine canonical records;
- no execution content or completion claim appears.

Then apply the exact disposition profile:

- `READY_FOR_CONTRACT`: candidate viability, every material delta, independent
  authority, selection, realization, strict-dominance result, and pressure treatment
  are true; a selected `D` carries the outcome and an `A` assures it; every applicable
  concern and gate is closed by its exact canonical owner; every non-applicable result
  has evidence; every observable claim has complete direct assurance; every applicable
  durable/ADR transition and `H` is complete and exactly projected; and Contract
  Interface introduces no decision or forces the future contract author to invent one.
- `DESIGN_NOT_REQUIRED`: existing `R/E` evidence closes the exact outcome and proves
  each existing or non-applicable architecture/gate result; Candidate Analysis resolves
  to no required design; and no Decision Register, selected `D`, or ready-only handoff
  is manufactured.
- `BLOCKED`: Candidate Analysis resolves to the complete canonical `B` set; only records
  valid before those blockers are present; and any optional partial Readiness Matrix
  contains only evaluated closure plus the matching failed or unresolved gate and
  blocker set, exactly as the schema disposition permits.

Any contradiction, orphaned semantic record, false source, incomplete transition,
proxy-only oracle, disproportionate mechanism, or invented handoff decision blocks,
regardless of lint success or apparent completeness.
